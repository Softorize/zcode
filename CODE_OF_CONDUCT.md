# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project expects respectful, professional collaboration.

Examples of behavior that contribute to a good environment:

- constructive technical feedback
- clear, evidence-based disagreement
- respect for different experience levels and backgrounds
- assuming good intent while still being direct about problems

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- personal attacks or insulting language
- doxxing or sharing private information without permission
- repeated bad-faith disruption of discussions, reviews, or issue threads

## Scope

This applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code review, discussions, and other project-managed communication channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or moderate content and may restrict participation for conduct that violates this policy.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project lead
through the [maintainer profile](https://github.com/gumruyanzh). Do not include
sensitive personal information in a public issue.
